## Experimental Protocols

### Protocol 1: Protein Hydration Shift

**Objective:** Test if water molecules shift α-helix ψ angle toward -42°

**Method:**
1. Select high-resolution protein structures (< 1.0 Å resolution)
2. Crystallize in two conditions:
   - **Hydrated:** Normal crystallization buffer
   - **Dehydrated:** Low humidity, cryoprotectant
3. Solve structures to < 1.0 Å resolution
4. Extract ψ angles for all α-helix residues
5. Compare distributions:
   - **Prediction:** Hydrated ψ shifted +3° toward -42°
   - **Null hypothesis:** No shift (both distributions centered at -45°)

**Expected Result:**
- Hydrated: ψ_peak = -42.5° ± 1°
- Dehydrated: ψ_peak = -45.0° ± 1°
- Shift: Δψ = +2.5° ± 1.5° (toward -42°)

**Statistical Power:**
- Need ~100 structures per condition
- α = 0.05, power = 0.95
- Effect size: d = 0.5 (medium)

### Protocol 2: Quantum Hall 25/32 State

**Objective:** Observe new FQHE state at ν = 25/32

**Method:**
1. Fabricate ultra-clean GaAs/AlGaAs heterostructure
   - Mobility > 10⁷ cm²/Vs
   - Density n_s ≈ 3 × 10¹¹ cm⁻²
2. Cool to T < 20 mK in dilution refrigerator
3. Apply magnetic field B = 12-15 T
4. Measure Hall resistance R_H and longitudinal resistance R_xx
5. Sweep magnetic field to scan filling fractions
6. Look for plateau at ν = 25/32:
   - R_H = (h/e²) × (32/25) = 1.28 × (25812.807 Ω) = 33,040 Ω
   - R_xx → 0 (within noise)

**Expected Result:**
- Plateau at B ≈ 13.2 T (for n_s = 3 × 10¹¹ cm⁻²)
- Width: ΔB ≈ 0.2 T
- Activation gap: Δ ≈ 0.5 K

**Challenges:**
- Requires world-class sample quality
- May be masked by nearby states (3/4 = 0.75, 4/5 = 0.80)
- Needs careful background subtraction

The 25/32 binary-Platonic framework is **falsified** if:

1. **Protein hydration shift:** Δψ < 0° (shift away from -42°) with p < 0.01

4. **Primary rainbow:** Angle deviates from 42.000° by > 0.1° (systematic error)

### Weak Falsification (Revise Framework)
