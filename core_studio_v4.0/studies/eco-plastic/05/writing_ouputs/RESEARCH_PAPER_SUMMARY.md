## Synthesis Protocol: Detailed Procedure

### Materials & Equipment

**Reagents**:
- ε-Caprolactone (100 g, distilled)
- Titanium(IV) isopropoxide (0.2 wt%)
- Toluene (dry, 10 mL)
- Nitrogen (inert gas)

**Equipment**:
- 500 mL round-bottom flask
- Mechanical stirrer
- Thermometer (-10 to 300°C)
- Condenser with drying tube
- Vacuum pump (0.1-1 mbar capable)

### Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Monomer Preparation** (10 min)
   - Distill ε-caprolactone under reduced pressure to remove volatiles
   - Store under nitrogen until use

2. **Reactor Setup** (15 min)
   - Charge 100 g distilled ECL into 500 mL flask
   - Attach mechanical stirrer, condenser, N₂ inlet, thermometer
   - Establish positive nitrogen flow

3. **Catalyst Addition** (5 min)
   - Dissolve 0.2 wt% Ti(OiPr)₄ in 10 mL dry toluene
   - Add dropwise via syringe under stirring
   - Stir at room temperature for 2 minutes

4. **Temperature Ramp** (30 min)
   - Slowly heat to 220°C over 20 minutes (avoid thermal runaway)
   - Apply vacuum (0.5 mbar) - remove toluene and residual moisture
   - Maintain 220-260°C range

5. **Polymerization** (4-8 hours)
   - Hold at target temperature under 0.1-1 mbar vacuum
   - Monitor viscosity increase via torque sensor
   - Periodically sample (1 mL) for GPC analysis
   - Continue until Mn ≈ 20,000-50,000 g/mol

6. **Devolatilization** (1 hour)
   - At final temperature, reduce to 0.1 mbar
   - Hold 1 hour under deep vacuum to remove oligomers and byproducts
   - Polymer should be clear and bubble-free

7. **Cooling & Extrusion** (30 min)
   - Cool to 180°C under nitrogen blanket
   - Extrude through 2-3 mm die into water-cooled trough
   - Collect strand, cool completely (5 minutes in water)

8. **Pelletization** (15 min)
   - Dry extruded strand with compressed air
   - Feed into laboratory pelletizer
   - Collect 3-4 mm pellets

### Characterization Protocol

**Gel Permeation Chromatography (GPC)**
- Solvent: Chloroform at 40°C
- Reference: Polystyrene standards
- Report: Mn, Mw, PDI (target Mn ≈ 20,000-30,000 g/mol)

**Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC)**
- Temperature range: -80°C to 200°C
- Heating rate: 10°C/min
- Expected: Tm ≈ 62°C, ΔHm ≈ 60-80 J/g (40-50% crystallinity)

**Tensile Testing (ASTM D638)**
- Sample: Type IV dumbbell (injection molded)
- Speed: 50 mm/min
- Expected: Tensile strength ≈ 50-60 MPa, Elongation ≈ 300-400%

**Biodegradability (ISO 14855-1)**
- Method: Aerobic composting at 58°C
- Duration: 28 days
- Expected: >40% mineralization (CO₂ evolution)
- Positive controls: Reference cellulose (>70%)
- Negative controls: PE film (<5%)
