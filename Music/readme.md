## 🔑 Key Findings & Metrics

The research yields several statistically significant, structurally explained insights into the intersection of prime number theory and harmonic structure:

### 🎼 Harmonic & Musical Structure
- **The Mod-144 Invariance Theorem**: Mersenne numbers ($2^p - 1$) modulo 144 always reduce to residues congruent to $7 \pmod{12}$ (the perfect fifth). Fermat numbers ($2^{2^k} + 1$) reduce to residues congruent to $5 \pmod{12}$ (the perfect fourth). This proves $12\text{-TET} = 7 + 5$ is a fundamental mathematical identity, invariant under modulus scaling.
- **Prime-Layer Harmonic Module**: Using Jaccard distance on threshold-10 proximity sets derived from the 4D mod-144 residue fingerprint achieves **$r = +0.8244$** for chord consonance, with perfect transposition invariance.
- **Pure Just-Intonation Exponent Vectors**: Achieve the strongest interval correlation in the study (**$r = +0.9613$**), proving consonance is fundamentally a property of prime factorization.

### 🌀 Topological & Coding-Theoretic Signatures
- **RLS Fusion Alignment**: Projecting UBP metrics onto the Radial Layer Spiral yields near-perfect correlations with angular prime density: Mean Angle (**$r = +0.9906$**), Anchor Distance (**$r = +0.9901$**), and Rotation Sign Changes (**$r = +0.9894$**).
- **4-Fold Symmetry**: The RLS prime density field exhibits exact 4-fold circular symmetry ($r = 0.984$ at 90° lag), inherited from the $D_4$ dihedral symmetry of the sum-of-two-squares lattice.
- **Complementary Prime Structures**: Primes $p \equiv 1 \pmod 4$ and $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$ exhibit fundamentally different, complementary angular structures (cross-correlation **$r = -0.40$**).
- **3D Structural Gain**: Introducing a temporal dimension (layer index, $\log n$, or iteration depth) to the RLS reveals **21% more structural variance** than 2D projections, confirming the temporal axis encodes genuine geometric information.

### ⚠️ Definitive Negative Results (Structural Limits)
- **The Golay 3-Distance Ceiling**: The Golay $[24, 12, 8]$ code inherently collapses harmonic differentiation into only two effective distance buckets (8 and 12). Chord-level differentiation via the coding layer alone is mathematically impossible ($r = 0.00$).

- **The Entropy Horizon**: The 4D residue fingerprint cannot sieve primes from composites ($r = -0.0094$). The primality signal lives exclusively in the error-correction layer (NRCI), not the harmonic prime layer.
- **Factor Size Estimation Failure**: Rotation spectral features encode the magnitude of a number ($\log n$), not its factorization structure, failing on general composites ($r < 0.20$).
