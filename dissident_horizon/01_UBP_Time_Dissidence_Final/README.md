## Key Findings

### 1. Frequency Coherence Hypothesis (Inverted)

**Original Hypothesis:** Synergistic interactions occur between nutrients with similar frequencies (low frequency ratio).

**Empirical Result:** **INVERTED**
- Synergies: mean ratio = 8.33 ± 14.91
- Antagonisms: mean ratio = 29.05 ± 32.98
- Effect size: Cohen's d = -0.809 (large effect)

**Interpretation:** Frequency mismatch is a mechanism for antagonism, not a barrier to synergy.

### 2. Biological Coherence Deficit

**Cosmological δ-deficit (Time study):** 0.15%  
**Biological δ-deficit (This study):** 40.58%  
**Ratio:** 270.56×

**Interpretation:** Dissidence is scale-dependent. Biology operates at a vastly higher deficit level than cosmology, suggesting different mechanisms or computational costs at different scales of reality.

### 3. Dissident States and Temporal Traps

**Identified:** 19 nutrients with NRCI < 0.259 (one standard deviation below mean)

**Top dissidents:** Aluminum, Lead, Curcumin, Chromium, Manganese, Vanadium, Cadmium, CoQ10, Non-heme Iron, Tin

**Time dilation:** ~95.5% slower internal time flow

**Interpretation:** These nutrients are "temporally trapped," making them difficult to process or eliminate from biological systems.
