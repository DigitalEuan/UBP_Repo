  * **Mechanism:** Calculate the **Ricci Curvature** or **Gaussian Curvature** along the resulting path or manifold.

  * **Defense Value:** This links coherence to the mathematical concepts of **network stability** and **influence**, which are widely used in computational biology and cheminformatics.
