### 1. Refine $D_6$ Composition Rules

The current model assumes Dependency Depth ($D_6$) is simply additive for composition, but this **breaks down** for complex derived operators like $\sin(x)$ and $\exp(x)$.

*   **Action:** Study the non-linear interactions, cancellation effects, and potential optimization that occur during the composition of derived operators to develop a **non-linear $D_6$ composition model**. This refinement is crucial for accurate coherence prediction of non-primitive functions.

### 2. Resolve the Y-Scaling Formula

The direct relationship between Hamming Weight (HW) and NRCI, governed by the Y-constant, is currently unreliable, showing a **sign error** and a weak correlation ($R^2 = 0.28$).

*   **Action:** Investigate alternative formulations, such as **layer-weighted Hamming metrics**, to find a stronger correlation between the 24-bit OffBit structure and NRCI, or conclusively determine why the D-variable model ($R^2=0.88$) is superior to the HW model. This must be resolved before Version 3.6 is released.
